Abstract : In many collaborative projects, especially those that include complicated tasks or big teams, the process of team formation is an essential step. We use the Digital Bibliography Library Project(DBLP)
dataset, a widely-used bibliographic database in the field of computer science. Specifically, we utilizes DBLP author profiles, relevant skills and expertise, and then forms teams based on complementary skillsets. In this
work, we discuss numerous methods that have been created for putting teams together based on skills. We also discuss certain challenges and limitations associated with forming skill-based teams. To evaluate our
approach, we conducted experiments on different approaches and compared the performance of our algorithm against several baseline methods.
